# Glide privacy policy

_Last updated: November 23, 2026_

Glide - Smooth Auto Scroll ("Glide") is a Chrome extension that scrolls web pages automatically at a speed you choose.

## What Glide collects
Nothing. Glide does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information or browsing data. It has no analytics, no tracking, no ads and no accounts, and it never connects to any server.

## What Glide stores on your device
Glide saves three settings using Chrome's local extension storage, on your computer only:
- your scroll speed
- whether the floating on-page control is shown
- whether scrolling pauses at the end of a page

These settings never leave your device. Uninstalling Glide deletes them.

## Permissions
- **activeTab** lets Glide work on the current tab only after you click its icon or press one of its keyboard shortcuts.
- **scripting** inserts the scrolling script and floating control into that tab.
- **storage** saves the settings listed above.

Glide does not read, record or send the contents of the pages you visit.

## Changes
If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
